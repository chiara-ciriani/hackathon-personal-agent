The problem · San Francisco

Sound stresses me out.

For many autistic people, some people with ADHD, and others sensitive to sound, noisy places can make it hard to stay.

But maps can't tell us how a place sounds right now.

The solution

A sound layer for personal agents.

Quiet Compass gives an agent fresh context to help you choose a quieter place or route—while keeping your plans and preferences in mind.

Tools the agent can call
query_sound_map
Read released, consented sound summaries.
find_suitable_places
Match places against a person's stated needs. Places with no measurement stay unknown.
submit_observation
Contribute a consented sound summary.
How we do it

Measure. Share. Decide.

01

Measure

The phone turns nearby sound into a short summary.

02

Share

With permission, share an approximate location and recent reading.

03

Decide

The agent checks nearby readings against your destination and travel time.

People choose whether to contribute. The phone shares a sound summary—not raw audio. A contribution opens shared queries for 15 minutes.
